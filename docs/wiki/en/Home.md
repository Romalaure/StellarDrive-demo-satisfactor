# Stellar Industries

Ever wanted to turn your ship into a factory? Stellar Industries brings everything you need to build a real production line to StellarDrive: machines that dig ore, smelt it, shape it and assemble it, generators to keep them running, and ways to move items from one machine to the next, and even from one ship to another.

You start out with a workshop, a smelter and a few iron ingots… and you end up with supercomputers and a particle accelerator.
