# Getting started

Here is how to set up your very first factory. It takes about ten minutes.

## 1. Unlock the first machines
Head to the research table. Once you have *Power Generation*, the **Industrie** branch shows up: research **Basic Industry** (20 iron and 10 copper). That unlocks the [Workshop](Machine-7601), the [Smelter](Machine-7602) and the [Extractor](Machine-7606), all in the "Industrie" build tab (production machines) and the "Industrie+" tab (power, extraction, logistics).

## 2. Bring power
Place a wood generator and your two machines. Every machine has its power socket on the back, in the small yellow and black box: run a power cable to the generator. Until it is plugged in, the machine tells you so ("Unpowered") when you look at it.

## 3. Start producing
Open the smelter (use key) and drop some iron ore in: a few seconds later the first ingots come out. Put those ingots in the workshop and it turns them into iron plates. Rather have rods than plates? Look at the workshop and press **N** to switch recipes.

Don't worry about your resources: a machine only takes its ingredients at the very end of a cycle, and only if it has room to store the result. Nothing gets lost.

## 4. Automate
Hauling ore by hand gets old fast. Place an [Extractor](Machine-7606) within 6 m of a rock: it pulls the ore out without ever using the rock up. The scanner (**K** key) shows the rocks around you.

With the *Item Lines* mod you can then link machines together through their item ports (on the back, next to the power socket): each machine sends out what it makes and only takes what it needs. It's all explained in [Logistics](Logistique).

## 5. Think bigger
Each new research is paid with what you already make: plates and rods first, then screws and cables, then rotors… That's what pulls you up the chain. See [Research](Recherche).

> Tip: look at a machine to see what it is doing, and press **F7** to check your whole factory at a glance.
