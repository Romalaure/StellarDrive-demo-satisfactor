# Power

The machines plug into the game's power grid like any other part: same cables, same generators.

## What the machines use
A machine only draws power while it is working. Its wattage is on its page (see [Machines](Machines)). If the grid can't keep up, every machine slows down a little instead of stopping dead, and the panel says "not enough power".

## Making power
- The game's wood generator gives 4,000 W.
- The [Combustion Generator](Machine-7607) goes up to 6,000 W. It burns logs, [biomass bricks](Objet-7714), [biomass](Objet-7729) or [charcoal](Objet-7728), and it is thrifty: it only burns what the grid actually uses.

## Saving some for later
The [Accumulator](Machine-7613) holds up to 60 MJ. When your generators have power to spare it charges up; as soon as the grid runs short, it takes over. Its charge is kept in your save.

## Wiring
Each machine's power socket is on the back, in the yellow and black box. A generator has a single socket: to power several machines, go through the game's power splitters.
