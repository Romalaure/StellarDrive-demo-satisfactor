# Power

The machines plug into the game's power grid like any other part: same cables, same generators.

## What the machines use
A machine only draws power while it is working. Its wattage is on its page (see [Machines](Machines)). If the grid can't keep up, every machine slows down a little instead of stopping dead, and the panel says "not enough power".

## Making power
- The game's wood generator gives 4,000 W.
- The [Biomass Burner](Machine-7615) is the small early-game generator: 1,500 W, it burns the same fuels as the combustion generator and only costs iron and copper.
- The [Combustion Generator](Machine-7607) goes up to 6,000 W. It burns logs, [biomass bricks](Objet-7714), [biomass](Objet-7729) or [charcoal](Objet-7728), and it is thrifty: it only burns what the grid actually uses.

- The [Stellar Reactor](Machine-7617) is the late-game power plant: 25 kW from [stellar fuel rods](Objet-7754), 5 minutes each at full power. Every spent rod leaves a [stellar waste](Objet-7755): take it out (by hand or with an item line), or the reactor stops for lack of room. The [Quantum Encoder](Machine-7618) recycles that waste into stellar matter.

## Saving some for later
The [Accumulator](Machine-7613) holds up to 60 MJ. When your generators have power to spare it charges up; as soon as the grid runs short, it takes over. Its charge is kept in your save.

## Wiring
Each machine's power socket is on the back, in the yellow and black box. The [Radar Tower](Machine-7622) has none: it needs no power.

## Power poles
Poles (the "Industrie+" tab) carry cables above the ground. All the sockets of a pole are on the same grid: plug a generator into one, machines into the others.

| Pole | Height | Connections | Research |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power Pole Mk1 | 2 m | 4 | *Power Lines* |
| Power Pole Mk2 | 3 m | 8 | *High-Voltage Lines* |
| Power Pylon Mk3 | 4 m | 12 | *High-Voltage Lines* | A generator has a single socket: to power several machines, go through the game's power splitters.
